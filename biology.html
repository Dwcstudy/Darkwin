Cell Biology

Structure, biomolecules, and division

The cell is the basic unit of life. Organelles separate functions and maintain internal organization through membranes and transport systems.

Core ideaStructure supports function across every level of organization
  • Mitochondria produce ATP through cellular respiration.
  • Ribosomes translate messenger RNA into proteins.
  • Mitosis maintains chromosome number; meiosis produces genetically varied gametes.
02

Human Physiology

Integrated body systems

Human physiology explains how systems maintain exchange, transport, coordination, movement, and internal stability.

Core linkStructure → process → response → homeostasis
  • Circulatory and respiratory systems support gas exchange and transport.
  • Neural and endocrine control coordinate responses at different speeds.
  • Kidneys regulate water, ions, waste removal, and blood composition.
03

Genetics & Evolution

Inheritance, variation, and selection

Genetics traces information encoded in DNA; evolution explains changes in populations across generations.

Revision keyGenotype → transcription → protein → phenotype
  • Alleles are alternative forms of a gene.
  • Inheritance patterns follow meiotic segregation and independent assortment.
  • Natural selection changes allele frequencies through differential survival and reproduction.
04

Ecology & Diversity

Organisms and ecosystems

Ecology studies interactions among organisms and their environment, including energy flow and nutrient cycling.

Core processSunlight → producers → consumers → decomposers
  • Only a limited fraction of energy transfers between trophic levels.
  • Niches describe how species use resources and interact.
  • Biodiversity supports ecosystem stability and ecological services.
